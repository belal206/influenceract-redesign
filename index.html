05 / Before you get started

Your questions. Clear answers.

Still have questions? Reach our team at contactus@influenceract.com.

01Does it cost anything to join?+

Yes. Creating a profile and joining the network is free. Campaign participation, deliverables and any applicable terms are shared before you accept a brief.

02Can I join with a small following?+

No. The right fit is not decided by follower count alone. Audience relevance, engagement quality, content and campaign requirements all help determine suitability.

03How are campaign opportunities shared?+

After your profile is reviewed, the team may contact you about briefs that fit your niche, platform, audience and location. You can review deliverables, timelines and commercials before accepting.

04When will I receive campaign payment?+

Payment is processed after the agreed deliverables are submitted and approved, according to the payment schedule and terms shared in that campaign brief.

05Can I create content in my language?+

Yes. Regional-language creators are welcome. However, requirements depend on each campaign, and briefs specifying the language and target regional audience.
